Your dashboard shows an order for ten shares. How many actually traded? Start with the evidence you have.

- [Answer a cancellation ticket](/guides/check-an-order#read-the-filled-quantity-after-cancellation). The customer has the order status. Find the executed quantity before you reply.
- [Recover an order after a lost response](/guides/check-an-order#recover-the-order-after-a-lost-response). Your app saved its client order ID. Use it to find the original request.
- [Run the offline lab](/quickstart). Inspect three snapshots of one order with Node.js, or [compare them in your browser](/quickstart#compare-the-next-two-snapshots). No account or keys required.

On an Alpaca Trading API paper order, `qty` is the quantity requested, `filled_qty` is the total executed so far, and `status` records the stage the order has reached.
